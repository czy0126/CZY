p.4  Electrical characteristics
     "ESR vs. frequency" is a log-log plot: frequency 100 Hz to 1 MHz on x,
     impedance / ESR 0.001 to 100 ohm on y.  The plotted parts are
     A720V477M002 (2 V) and A720V226M035 (35 V).  The 2.5 V 470 uF part used in
     this design is NOT plotted, and the plot carries no tabulated values.
     The document contains no ESR-vs-temperature and no ESR-vs-DC-bias curve.
     The only other curve is capacitance vs frequency for the same two parts.

p.6  Ripple current / ripple voltage
     Pmax = 410 mW at 25 C for a +20 C rise, case V / EIA 7343-21.
     I(max) = sqrt(Pmax / R), with R the ESR at the frequency of interest.
     The rated ripple current in the characteristics table is therefore a
     100 kHz number, not a 200 kHz number.

p.7  Table 2 -- recommended land dimensions, case V / EIA 7343-21, millimetres,
     three density levels, values in the order W L S V1 V2:
       Level A (maximum land protrusion)       2.55 2.77 3.67 10.22 5.60
       Level B (nominal, recommended reflow)   2.43 2.37 3.87  9.12 5.10
       Level C (minimum, IPC-7351 test first)  2.33 1.99 4.03  8.26 4.84
     This is an OEM-recommended land table.  It is not yet an approved EDA
     footprint: the W/L/S/V1/V2 mapping has to be read off the figure on the
     same page, and the density level, courtyard and polarity marking are
     engineering decisions.  Level C is explicitly conditional on IPC-7351
     qualification testing.

p.7  Reverse voltage: allowable transient reverse voltage is 60% of rated at
     25 C, 50% at 55 C, 40% at 85 C, 30% at 105 C.  Continuous reverse
     operation is not allowed at any of these levels.

Why this file exists: the P0 RFQ asks for ESR at 200 kHz, 2.5 V bias, operating
temperature and end of life.  The series answers only the last one, and only as
a certified LIMIT (ESR <= 2.0 x initial after 2,000 h at 105 C / rated voltage).
The other three stay UNKNOWN; a 200 kHz number must not be inferred from the
100 kHz plot of a different voltage rating.
