Applicability to Q41 REG crowbar bench:
- Existing PCB footprint Package_TO_SOT_SMD:SOT-23 and KiCad Q_NMOS_GSD
  have the nominal G=1/S=2/D=3 mapping for AO3400A, and 30 V rating.
- Datasheet guarantees <0.1 ohm at VGS=2.5 V under its specified conditions
  at 25 C, so it is a useful purchasing/first-article candidate.
- This does NOT guarantee RDS(on) <0.1 ohm at elevated temperature or at
  an arbitrary current, nor does IDM=30 A guarantee 14.8 W pulse survivability.
- Circuit gate drive under different fault input amplitude, temperature,
  current pulse shape, source shift, repeated fault and REG charging current
  still requires measured VGS and a OEM SOA/thermal review.
- Selection improves procurement traceability; NOT authorized for fabrication
  or power-stage fault shutdown until real test data and approved reviews.
